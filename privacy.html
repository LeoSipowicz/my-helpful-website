Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

At A Helpful Website, we take your privacy seriously. This policy describes how we handle your information when you use our tools and browse our site.

Data Processing

We never see your data. All processing happens locally in your browser. Our image tools use the HTML5 Canvas API, while our text, developer, and security tools use JavaScript. When you use any tool on this site - whether uploading an image, typing text, pasting JSON, or generating a password - your data stays on your computer. It is not sent to any server, stored in any database, or accessible to us or any third party. Once you close or refresh the page, the data is permanently discarded from memory.

Information We Collect

We use Google Analytics to collect anonymized usage data such as page views, visit duration, and browser type. This helps us understand which tools are most popular and how to improve the site. Google Analytics does not receive any file contents, text data, or personally identifiable information. You can learn more about how Google handles Analytics data at Google's partner privacy policy.

We also use Google AdSense to display advertisements. AdSense may use cookies to serve relevant ads based on your browsing history. No file contents or text data is shared with AdSense.

Cookies

This site uses cookies for Google Analytics and AdSense. These cookies are used to measure site usage and to personalize advertisements. You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings. Blocking cookies will not affect your ability to use our tools.

Third-Party Services

The following third-party services are used on this website:

  • Google Analytics - for aggregated visitor statistics
  • Google AdSense - for advertising

Each of these services operates under its own privacy policy. We do not share any file contents, text data, or personal information with these services beyond what is necessary for their standard operation.

Data Retention

Since we do not collect or store any files, text, or personal data (beyond anonymized analytics), there is nothing to retain. Google Analytics data is retained according to Google's standard retention policies.

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete any personal data held about you. Since we do not collect personal data, these rights are inherently fulfilled. For questions about privacy, please contact us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
