Regex Tester

Test a regular expression against your own text and see matches highlighted as you type. Inspect capture groups, try a replacement, and copy the result. Everything runs in your browser, so your text is never sent to a server.

    Common patterns

    Regex cheat sheet
    .
    Any character except a newline
    \d \w \s
    Digit, word character, whitespace
    * + ?
    Zero or more, one or more, zero or one
    {2,5}
    Between 2 and 5 repetitions
    [abc]
    One character from the set
    [^abc]
    One character not in the set
    (abc)
    Capture group, referenced as $1
    (?:abc)
    Non-capturing group
    ^ $
    Start and end of the text or line
    \b
    Word boundary
    a|b
    Either a or b
