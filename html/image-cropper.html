How to Crop Images Online

Cropping is one of the most common image editing tasks. Whether you need to remove unwanted edges, focus on a subject, adjust the aspect ratio for social media, or prepare a product photo for a listing, cropping is usually the first step. Our free Image Cropper lets you do this instantly in your browser without uploading anything to a server.

Why Crop Images?

Photographers crop to improve composition using the rule of thirds. Social media managers crop photos to match platform-specific aspect ratios — Instagram posts prefer 1:1 or 4:5, Twitter cards use 1.91:1, and LinkedIn banners are 1584x396. E-commerce sellers crop product images to remove backgrounds and create clean, consistent thumbnails. Web developers crop screenshots to highlight specific UI elements. And everyday users simply want to cut out irrelevant parts of a photo before sharing it with friends.

Full-Resolution Cropping

Unlike some online tools that crop the preview image at screen resolution, our tool crops the original file at its full native resolution. This means if you upload a 4000x3000 pixel image and select a small region, the cropped output will still be high resolution and suitable for printing or further editing. The preview canvas shows your image at a comfortable viewing size, but the actual crop operation works on the original pixel data.

Tips for Best Results

For the sharpest results, start with the highest resolution original you have. If you plan to crop and then resize, always crop first and resize second — this preserves the most detail. If you need a specific output size after cropping, use our Image Resizer tool after downloading your cropped image. For web use, follow up with our Image Compressor to reduce file size without visible quality loss.

How to Use This Tool

Upload your image using the file picker above. Click and drag on the image to draw a selection rectangle. The dimensions of your selection are shown below the image. Click Crop Selection to apply the crop and see the result. If you are not happy with the selection, click Reset Selection to try again. When you are satisfied, click Download Cropped Image to save the result to your computer. Your image never leaves your browser.
