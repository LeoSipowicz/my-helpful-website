Image Cropper

Upload an image, choose an aspect ratio, and drag to select the area you want to keep. Drag inside the selection to move it. Presets like 1:1, 4:3, and 16:9 lock the shape, or leave it on Free for any rectangle. The crop is applied at full resolution, and all processing happens locally in your browser.
